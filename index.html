About Leslie Simon

The human judgment behind the house.

Leslie Simon has built her career at the intersection of business, talent and culture. Before moving into recruiting and talent strategy, she worked across merchandising, product design, global licensing and brand partnerships. That firsthand operating experience shaped her understanding of what companies need from their people and sparked her greatest professional passion: connecting organizations with exceptional talent.

Today, Leslie partners with entrepreneurial businesses, growing companies and global brands to make critical hires, strengthen leadership and build high-performing teams. Her recruiting experience spans consumer brands, fashion and retail, financial services, professional sports, healthcare and medical devices, technology, sustainability and emerging industries.

Beyond search, Leslie has built and scaled university recruiting platforms, internship programs and early-career initiatives. Now, as she experiences the transformation of work firsthand, she brings that same human-centered perspective to Future Ready Roles. Her conviction is clear: the human element cannot be replaced, but roles can be thoughtfully redesigned to pair human judgment, creativity and connection with the possibilities of AI.
